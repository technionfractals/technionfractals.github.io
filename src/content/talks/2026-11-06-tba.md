This talk will present a recent breakthrough by OpenAI on the absolute continuity of Bernoulli convolutions. We will discuss the published proof of the complete characterization of singularity and absolute continuity, and outline the strategy for establishing singularity for Bernoulli convolutions associated with the reciprocals of Salem numbers of degree four.
