We explain OpenAI’s proof of the exact overlaps conjecture for the dimension of self-similar measures, listed as Result 148 in its catalog. The key input is an unexpected inequality relating pair capacities to the gap between Shannon and partition entropies within Varjú’s framework (2019) for quantitative entropy increase. The proof builds more directly on ideas of Varjú (2019) and Breuillard–Varjú (2019), while the multiscale analysis of entropy increase under convolution originates in Hochman’s work (2014).
